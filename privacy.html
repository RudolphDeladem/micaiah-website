MICAIAH AI

Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Summary

Micaiah AI is designed so that your reflections stay yours. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Your journal and interpretations are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The reflections you write or speak, the insights generated for them, the dreams and prophetic words you submit and their interpretations, your optional first name, and your app preferences (such as reminder time and Bible translation) are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data and it is not uploaded to our servers except as described below.

Voice input

If you choose to speak a reflection, the app uses your device's built-in speech recognition service (Apple Speech on iOS, the system speech service on Android) to convert your voice to text. Where your device supports it, recognition runs on-device. If it does not, your operating system provider may process the audio according to its own privacy policy. Micaiah AI does not record, store, or upload your audio. Microphone and speech recognition permissions are only requested when you tap the microphone, and you can revoke them at any time in your device settings.

Optional cloud insights

By default, insights are generated entirely on your device. In some versions of the app you may choose to turn on "Cloud insights" in Settings. Only if you explicitly opt in, the text of each reflection you submit is sent over an encrypted connection to the Micaiah AI insight service, which uses Anthropic's Claude AI model to write a personalised reflection. The text is used only to generate that response: Micaiah AI does not store it after the response is returned, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models. Anthropic processes the text as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not permit training on it; Anthropic may retain it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. You can turn cloud insights off at any time, and the app will return to on-device insights.

Dream Interpretation and Prophetic Word

Where the app offers Dream Interpretation and Prophetic Word, these always use the Micaiah AI service. The first time you use them, the app asks for your agreement. Each time you submit a dream or a prophetic word, its text is sent over an encrypted connection to the service, where Anthropic's Claude AI writes a biblical reflection on it. As with cloud insights, Micaiah AI does not store the text after the response is returned, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models. To apply monthly allowances, the service keeps a count of how many interpretations each install has requested this month, linked only to a random install ID created by the app — not to your name, email, or device identifiers.

Subscriptions

Micaiah Plus is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which process your payment under their own privacy policies; we never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (such as the product, purchase dates, and whether the subscription is active) linked to the app's random install ID. We use this only to unlock Micaiah Plus features.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers and do not collect a device token.

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the content you choose is passed to the app you select (for example, Messages). Nothing is shared without your action.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the Journal or the Interpret tab, or erase everything using Settings → Delete all data, which also replaces the app's random install ID. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device. Monthly interpretation counts on our service are discarded after the month ends.

Children

Micaiah AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for material changes, notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
