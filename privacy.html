MICAIAH AI

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Summary

Micaiah AI is designed so that your reflections stay yours. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Insights, dream interpretations and prophetic-word reflections are all created on your device, and your journal is stored only on your device. We do not collect any of your data.

Information stored on your device

The reflections you write or speak, the insights generated for them, the dreams and prophetic words you write and their interpretations, your optional first name, and your app preferences (such as reminder time and Bible translation) are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data, and the app does not upload it anywhere.

How insights and interpretations are created

Micaiah AI uses a Scripture library built into the app to prepare insights, dream interpretations and prophetic-word reflections entirely on your device. The text you write is never sent to us or to any AI service, and it is never used to train AI models.

Voice input

If you choose to speak a reflection, the app uses your device's built-in speech recognition service (Apple Speech on iOS, the system speech service on Android) to convert your voice to text. Where your device supports it, recognition runs on-device. If it does not, your operating system provider may process the audio according to its own privacy policy. Micaiah AI does not record, store, or upload your audio. Microphone and speech recognition permissions are only requested when you tap the microphone, and you can revoke them at any time in your device settings.

Purchases

This version of Micaiah AI is free and has no in-app purchases. If we add optional online features or subscriptions in a future version, the app will explain them and ask before any text leaves your device, and we will update this policy before that version is released.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers and do not collect a device token.

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the content you choose is passed to the app you select (for example, Messages). Nothing is shared without your action.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the Journal or the Interpret tab, or erase everything using Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Micaiah AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for material changes, notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
