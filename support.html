Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Micaiah works without sign-up. Your journal lives only on your device.

How do I back up my journal?
Open Settings → Export journal to save or send a copy of all your reflections.

How do I delete my data?
Delete a single reflection from its page, or erase everything with Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all data.

Voice input isn't working.
Allow microphone and speech recognition access for Micaiah in your device settings. On Android, speech recognition requires the Google app or an on-device speech service.

Which Bible translation is used?
Scripture is quoted from the King James Version (public domain).

I saw an insight that seemed wrong.
Tap "Report this insight" at the bottom of the insight, or email us. Your report helps keep Micaiah faithful and safe.
